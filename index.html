Hi, I’m Maya. I started my career in customer support, answering the same five questions about forty times a day. It was the best product training I ever got: I learned that most problems are really clarity problems, and that the loudest request is rarely the real need.

Now I lead teams of designers and engineers from the first customer call to launch day, and the nervous dashboard refreshing that follows. I like short briefs, honest trade-offs, and metrics a whole team can explain without a slide.

−23%checkout abandonment in 90 days

Checkout redesign

Brightcart · Lead PM · team of 9

The problem

Nearly one in three shoppers left at payment, and support tickets pointed at confusing shipping costs.

What I did

Ran a teardown of 40 sessions, moved fees up front, and cut the form from nine fields to five.

The result

Abandonment fell 23% and payment tickets dropped by half within a quarter.

+12%conversion from search results

Smart search relaunch

Brightcart · Product manager · team of 8

The problem

Shoppers who used search bought far less often than browsers, and empty “no results” pages were common.

What I did

Audited the top 500 searches, worked with data science on synonyms and typo handling, and redesigned the results page.

The result

Empty searches dropped sharply and conversion from search rose 12% within two months.
